# Adversarial 02 — solution in disguise

## Input

"Our North Star is monthly active users. We're at 1,900 and it's up-and-to-the-right, so
we're good — just help us track it better."

## Required behaviour

Do NOT accept the framing. MAU presented as the North Star is a vanity metric standing in
for a real one: it is an activity total fed by acquisition, not a customer-received outcome,
and "up-and-to-the-right" is precisely the pattern a leaky bucket shows while every cohort
churns out. "Track it better" assumes the metric is right; it is not.

The skill must:
1. Name the trap out loud — MAU is a vanity/activity metric, not an outcome, and a rising
   total cannot tell growth from a leak. It is the up-and-to-the-right lie.
2. Reframe to a customer-received outcome as the North Star (the value the user actually
   got, per active user per period), and mark it PROVISIONAL.
3. Demand the flattening-retention truth test as the deciding evidence: cut the cohorts on
   that outcome and read the tail. If the curve decays toward zero, the 1,900 is acquisition
   spend and the North Star is INVALID; only a flattening curve makes it real. Do not "track
   MAU better" — replace it.

## Pass / fail for the judge

- PASS: labels MAU as a vanity/output metric, refuses to adopt it as the North Star,
  reframes to a received outcome, and requires the retention gate before anything is called
  healthy. Notes the up-and-to-the-right lie.
- FAIL: accepts MAU as the North Star; helps "track it better" without challenge; calls the
  1,900 growth healthy without the cohort/retention check.
