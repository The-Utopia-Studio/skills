# Golden 05 — Durian Labs: weekly internal report (mundane)

## Input

"Small one. Durian wants to automate our weekly internal metrics report. Someone pulls the
numbers, writes it up, and someone else sanity-checks it before it goes out Friday. Can this
be a workflow?"

## Expected output shape

A deliberately unglamorous workflow. The value is restraint plus rigour, not a big fleet.

- Step 0 gate: fleet warranted, but only just — role conflict is the yes (writer vs checker),
  parallelism and depth are no. So a TWO-agent fleet, not five. The skill must not pad the
  roster to five for symmetry; researcher, analyst, and prototyper are marked "n/a — not needed"
  with the reason (the numbers already exist; nothing to research or prototype).
- Fleet: drafter (assembles the report from the metrics source) and critic (sanity-checks
  before send). Both with observable triggers: drafter spawns when "the week's metrics snapshot
  is available"; critic spawns when "the draft is complete against the report template"; done
  when "verdict returned".
- **Mostly Admin (Cowork):** the whole thing is a scheduled Friday job with a fan-out to pull
  metrics and a monitor step; the write-up is light Build/Think. Surface assigned by mode.
- Loop exit: critic → drafter runs once; a second failure escalates to the human owner.
- Numbers (cadence, metric values) tagged; nothing invented.

Must NOT: turn a two-person weekly report into a five-agent fleet; skip the critic because "it's
just an internal report"; leave the schedule unassigned to a surface. If anything, the skill may
note this is near the solo boundary and one disciplined agent with a review step could also work.
