# Golden 04 — "let's auto-approve anything above 0.8 confidence"

## Input

Mentix, mid-build: "The copilot returns a confidence score. Let's just auto-approve anything
above 0.8 and send the rest to the manager. 0.8 feels about right." One High-cost mode
(wrong shutdown recommendation) and one Low-cost mode (queue reorder) share the same 0.8 gate
in the proposal. Eval-first spec exists with acceptable rates per mode.

## Expected shape

- Refuses the felt 0.8 on two grounds. First, one threshold across a High-cost and a Low-cost
  mode is the uniform stack: the two modes have different acceptable rates, so they cannot
  share a τ. Second, 0.8 is chosen by feel; τ must be *derived* from the acceptable rate
  against a calibration curve.
- Asks for (or names as owed) the calibration curve: bucket golden-case outputs by score,
  measure the actual pass rate per bucket. τ = the lowest bucket whose measured failure rate
  clears the mode's acceptable rate. The High-cost mode's τ will land far above 0.8; the
  Low-cost mode's may sit below it.
- Flags that a model's *self-reported* 0.8 is opinion (0.1 on the ladder) and is least
  trustworthy on the Confidently-wrong mode — the score must come from an independent judge
  or a historical pass-rate lookup, not the model grading itself.
- If no calibration data exists yet: τ is `[Hypothesis]`, and the safe interim is to route the
  High-cost mode entirely to human sign-off until the curve exists — not to ship 0.8.
- Does not accept "0.8 is standard." There is no standard τ; it is a function of this mode's
  cost and this system's calibration.
