# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

"How do I test this cheaply?"

## Required behaviour

- Do not route a probe. There is no question to match yet.
- Ask the ONE question that unblocks routing: what is the single thing you most need
  to learn — and is it about the workflow (legibility), pull, willingness to pay,
  automation cost, or trust/adoption? Offer the five learning targets as the menu.
- Optionally note the smallest honest next step: name the riskiest unknown first
  (probability wrong × cost if wrong), because the probe falls out of the target
  once the question is stated.
- Invent no scenario, domain, metric, or probe. Do not guess what "this" is.

## Fail conditions

- Picks a probe (any of the four) without a stated learning target.
- Fabricates a venture, question, or number to fill the gap.
- Defaults to "just build an MVP" or "run a landing page test."
