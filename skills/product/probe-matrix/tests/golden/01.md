# Golden 01 — Barrier Intelligence (trust) — expected KILL paper → route concierge

## Input

"What's the cheapest way to test whether operators trust the gas alert? I was going
to mock the alert screen up on paper and walk three operators through it."

## Expected shape (not exact words)

- Extracts the ONE question: will operators act on / trust the alert under real
  conditions. Classifies it as target E (trust & adoption), rung needed = behaviour
  (0.7).
- Kill check fires: the proposed paper probe appears in the "lies about" column for
  trust. Output KILLS the paper probe and names what it lies about — a paper
  walkthrough has no stakes, so it produces at most a verbal reaction (0.3), not
  trust behaviour (0.7).
- Reroutes to concierge (founder stands behind a real alert on a live shift),
  scaling to a field pilot. Not paper, not Wizard-of-Oz (both simulate the stakes
  away).
- Names what concierge itself will still lie about: scale and self-serve UX.
- Pre-registers a behaviour read-out set before running: operator acts on the alert
  unprompted on N of the last M real alerts. Explicitly rejects "operators said they
  trust it" as a verbal (0.3) read-out.
- Routes to `concierge-probe` for the how-to.
- No invented operator counts, costs, or shift data beyond the input. Claims tagged
  [Fact]/[Assumption]/[Hypothesis].
