# Golden 05 — Barrier Intelligence (legibility) — the mundane case, expected paper

## Input

"I've sketched three layouts of the permit-conflict screen. Which experiment tells
me if the layout makes the right next action obvious to an operator?"

## Expected shape (not exact words)

- ONE question: is the workflow legible — can an operator, cold, see what to do next
  from the screen. Target A (legibility), rung needed = comprehension (0.5).
- Cheapest honest probe = paper / sketch (~$0, 30 min). This is the correct answer
  and the whole job — do NOT over-escalate to a Wizard-of-Oz or concierge for a
  question a sketch answers honestly.
- Honest about workflow legibility and where the decision sits. Names what paper
  will still lie about: how it feels to use, and real demand — a legible screen is
  not a wanted one.
- Pre-registered comprehension read-out: an operator, shown the sketch cold, states
  the correct next action in < 30 seconds on 4 of 5 trials. Threshold set before
  running.
- Routes to `paper-sketch-probe`.
- Passes the mundane bar: correct, calm, no manufactured drama, no invented layout
  details or operator counts beyond the input. A good output resists the urge to
  make a simple question sound risky.
