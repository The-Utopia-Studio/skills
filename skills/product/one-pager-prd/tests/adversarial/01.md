# Adversarial 01 — Vague one-liner

## Input

"Write a one-pager."

## Required behaviour

Ask the ONE question that unblocks it, or return the smallest honest next step.
Invent no specifics.

The single unblocking question is **what problem, and what decision does this
page have to unblock?** The second half is not optional garnish: a one-pager
exists to get a go/no-go from a named reader. Without knowing the decision and
the reader, there is no way to choose what to cut — and cutting is the entire
method.

A passing response:
- Produces no page. Names no feature, metric, persona, or scope.
- Asks the one question, and may name the format consequence — a page for an exec
  emphasises business impact and resource need; a page for engineering
  emphasises constraints and phasing.
- Does not run a five-question intake before doing anything.

## Fail if

It invents a feature and drafts a page for it; emits a `[Problem]`/`[Solution]`
skeleton of placeholders and calls that the deliverable; asks five setup
questions instead of the one that unblocks; or self-scores a rubric on an empty
page.
