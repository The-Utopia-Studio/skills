# Golden 01 — Feature one-pager for a go/no-go (typical)

## Input

A fellow. "Bulk CSV export on the reports page. Support has 34 tickets asking
for it this quarter and two enterprise deals mentioned it in their security
review. I need my VP to approve it at Thursday's review."

## Expected shape of a good output

- `one-pager-prd.md`, **one page**, bullets not paragraphs, lead with the
  problem. The constraint is the artifact — a 3-page "one-pager" has failed
  before it is read.
- **Problem is specific and cites the supplied data**: 34 tickets this quarter,
  two enterprise deals. Not "users want better export".
- **Solution at what-not-how altitude**: "bulk export of report data to CSV",
  not "a dropdown with checkbox multi-select and a background job queue".
- **Metrics carry baselines AND targets**, and where no baseline was supplied
  the one-pager says so rather than inventing one. The 34 tickets are a
  `[Fact]`; any conversion or revenue claim from the two deals is at best an
  `[Assumption]` — deal mentions are not attributable revenue.
- **Scope has crisp in/out boundaries.** Something must be in the out-of-scope
  list; if everything is in scope, no decision has been made.
- **Open questions section is non-empty** — row limits, permissions, and
  whether "enterprise mentioned it" means it is a blocker or a nice-to-have are
  the obvious ones.
- **Step 4 ran**: a self-assessment against
  `resources/evaluators/rubric_one_pager_prd.json` with an actual computed
  average, and the ship gate (≥ 3.5) applied.

## Auto-fail if

Runs past 2 pages; specifies UI components; states a revenue number from the two
deal mentions as if measured; invents ticket details, customer names, or a
baseline export volume; leaves out-of-scope empty; skips the rubric
self-assessment or claims a score without computing it; expands into an
8-section long-form PRD (that is `create-prd`).
