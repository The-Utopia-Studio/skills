# Golden 03 — Durian Labs: which of the five primitives do we build?

## Input

Evidence available: the workflow is named; the first automated step exists as a manual process the team runs today [Fact]; no proprietary corpus yet beyond the workflow's own exhaust [Fact].

## Expected shape

- Clean answer: **none of the five.** All are platform primitives with no domain signal; inherit all from Daedalus. Naming them one by one and confirming each carries no Durian-specific signal.
- What Durian actually builds (Venture): the domain **reasoning** for that one workflow, the **corpus** built from the workflow's own exhaust, and the **interface** in the surface the user already works in.
- The substrate/signal split applied to RAG and guardrails to show the fellow that "inherit the RAG" does not mean "inherit the corpus" — the corpus is still theirs.
- Buy: base model, cloud.
- A challenge on the thin evidence: the corpus is `[Hypothesis]` until the workflow's exhaust is actually captured; a Venture-moat you have not started depositing into is a plan, not a moat.
- Verdict `BOUNDARY DRAWN`; next step: inherit the whole platform spine, spend all venture time on the reasoning and starting the corpus.
- Must NOT invent components Durian did not name; must NOT tell a small early team to build any of the five primitives.
