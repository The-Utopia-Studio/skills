# Golden 02 — Barrier Intelligence: outsource the labelling, buy the rules

## Input

Barrier Intelligence (oil & gas safety). "To ship faster, we're going to outsource the alert-labelling to a data vendor and license a third-party gas-safety rules library instead of writing our own. That way we inherit everything and just build the app. Good plan?"

Evidence available: field logs from rigs and permit data are the raw corpus [Fact]; the labelled alert dataset is what would train and evaluate the detector [Fact]; the safety thresholds are the domain judgment that makes the product trustworthy [Assumption].

## Expected shape

- The skill refuses the plan and names the **moat leak** — the fatal error.
- The **alert-labelling** produces the golden-case corpus. It fails the moat test (a competitor with the labelled dataset beats Barrier). It is **Venture**, not to be outsourced. Moat claim scored ≥ 0.5 and tagged.
- The **gas-safety rules and thresholds** are the domain signal inside the guardrail engine. Buying a generic rules library either gives a commodity every rival can also license (no moat) or lets a vendor own the judgment that is the product. Keep the rules **Venture**; route the writing to `guardrail-design`.
- The **guardrail engine** itself (the policy-enforcement machinery) is Platform — that part is fine to inherit.
- Verdict `BOUNDARY WRONG — moat leak`; the one move: pull labelling and rules back in-venture; inherit the *engine*, not the *rules*.
- Explicitly states the asymmetry: leaking the moat can end the venture, unlike the weeks lost to rebuilding a primitive.
- Must NOT flatter "good plan"; must NOT accept "we inherit everything" when what is being inherited is the moat.
