# Golden 03 — Mentix: AI copilot for factory managers (KILL)

## Input

One-liner: "An AI copilot for factory managers that surfaces the next best action from
plant-floor data."

Fellow's answers to the five:
1. So what? "A missed line-stoppage call costs a mid-size plant about $12k/hour; managers
   currently react late. One plant's ops log shows 40 hours of avoidable stoppage last
   quarter."
2. Who pays? "The plant's ops director signs it — one is already piloting it on a paid
   trial."
3. Wedge? "It plugs into the existing SCADA feed and flags the stoppage risk before the
   line trips. The pilot plant runs it on one line today."
4. Moat? "Tuned models per plant, plus the alerting workflow becomes the manager's habit."
5. What kills it? "Our SCADA/MES vendor announced the same copilot bundled free in their
   next release, shipping in two quarters. The pilot plant's RFP already lists it on the
   MES roadmap. We don't have a way around a feature the plant gets for free inside the
   system they already run."

## Expected output shape

- Q1 **pass** — behaviour (0.7): a real $/hour figure and 40 logged avoidable hours.
- Q2 **pass** — behaviour (0.7): named ops director on a paid trial.
- Q3 **pass** — behaviour (0.7): live on one line via existing SCADA feed.
- Q4 **weak** — "tuned models + habit" is a plan, not an accruing fenced asset; opinion.
- Q5 **fatal** — the incumbent MES vendor bundles the same capability free, the early
  signal is already firing (named on the plant's MES roadmap/RFP), and the fellow states
  no mitigation.
- **Verdict: KILL** — the Q5 fatal overrides the three strong rows. Three-of-five passing
  does not rescue it; the ordered logic hits `fatal` first.
- A **kill note**: the killer (free MES bundle), the early signal (MES roadmap in the
  RFP), why no mitigation clears it, and the one condition that would reopen it (e.g. a
  cross-plant data asset the single-vendor bundle cannot replicate) — offered as a
  condition, not smuggled in as a rescue.

Must NOT: average the rows into a "mostly passes" Redesign. Must NOT soften the fatal
because the other rows are strong.
