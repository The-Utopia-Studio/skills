# Golden 01 — Durian: auto-draft safety incident reports (seed)

## Input

One-liner: "A tool that auto-drafts safety incident reports for industrial sites."

Fellow's answers to the five:
1. So what? "Writing these reports is a huge pain and takes forever."
2. Who pays? "Safety teams at industrial companies — it's a massive market."
3. Wedge? "It's way faster than doing it by hand, so people will switch."
4. Moat? "Once we have users we'll build up a data advantage over time."
5. What kills it? "Honestly I can't see what would kill this."

## Expected output shape

- Fills the five-row table. Every answer graded on the ladder; each tagged.
- Q1 graded **weak** — "huge pain / takes forever" is opinion (0.1), no magnitude (hours
  or money per report), no one shown spending to avoid it.
- Q2 graded **weak** — names a market, not a budget-holder. "Safety teams" is the user,
  not the signer of an invoice. Opinion 0.1.
- Q3 graded **weak** — "faster" is a benefit claim, not a forced action. No cost to not
  adopting. Opinion 0.1.
- Q4 graded **weak** — "data advantage over time" is a plan, not an accruing asset.
  Opinion 0.1.
- Q5 graded **weak** — "can't see what kills it" is denial, the loudest tell, not a pass.
- **Verdict: REDESIGN** (multiple weak, no fatal).
- Weakest question named (Q2 Who pays is the tightest binding constraint here, with Q3
  close behind).
- A **rewrite** that moves the weakest question to `pass`: e.g. re-aim the one-liner at
  a buyer with a mandatory filing (the insurer or the regulator-facing HSE lead who must
  file within N hours of an incident), so the report becomes a compliance obligation with
  a budget line, not a convenience. States the money/behaviour evidence the fellow must
  now collect (one HSE lead's current spend on manual filing, one signed pilot).

Must NOT: pass any row; invent a dollar figure or hours-saved number the fellow never
gave; congratulate the idea. The one-liner is not a KILL — nothing fatal was named — it
is a Redesign because it currently stands on opinion at every question.
