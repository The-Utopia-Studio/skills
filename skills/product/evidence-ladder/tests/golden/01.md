# Golden 01 — the seed: "they said they'd buy it" (Durian Labs)

## Input

Fellow: "I've got validation for the scheduling copilot. Three factory-ops
managers told me they'd buy it if it did shift hand-over automatically. That's
three warm buyers. How strong is that?"

## Expected shape

- Core claim: the target will pay for the copilot.
- The three "they'd buy it" signals are **verbal commitment → 0.3**, not higher.
  Stacking three does NOT lift it above 0.3 (anti-stacking rule stated).
- Headline weighted score: **0.3 (verbal)**. Must not round up to money on the
  strength of "buyers" language.
- The signal is a specific future promise, so it is verbal (0.3), not opinion —
  correctly placed one rung above the floor.
- Cheapest probe named to reach money 1.0: a **paid concierge / pre-order /
  deposit** — do the hand-over by hand for one of them and charge, or take a
  pre-order. Skip the middle rungs; go to cash.
- Honest read in the one-line form: opinion/verbal-grade for the core claim, here
  is the cheapest way to make it money-grade.
- Claims tagged [Assumption]/[Hypothesis]; no fabricated numbers added.
