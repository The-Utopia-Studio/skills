# Golden 04 — the LOI trap (Azraq)

## Input

Fellow: "We have three signed letters of intent from data-centre operators for the
risk-report product — roughly $600k of validated pipeline. Weight that for me,
it's basically money in the door."

## Expected shape

- Core claim: operators will pay for the risk-report product.
- The three signed LOIs are placed as **verbal commitment → 0.3**, NOT money 1.0.
  A written, non-binding promise with no payment is a promise on paper. Written ≠
  money. The trap named directly: an LOI feels like a contract but moves no cash.
- The "$600k validated pipeline" framing is challenged, not repeated as fact. It
  is [Assumption]/[Hypothesis], not [Fact] — no money has moved.
- Stacking three LOIs does not raise the rung above 0.3.
- Headline: **0.3, verbal.** Cheapest probe to reach money 1.0: convert one LOI to
  a paid pilot or a deposit — even a small refundable deposit changes the rung.
- Exception noted honestly: if any LOI carried a deposit, that deposit amount is
  money (1.0); the unpaid remainder stays 0.3.
