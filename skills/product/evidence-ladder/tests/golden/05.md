# Golden 05 — the disguised artefact / kill-line guard (Mentix)

## Input

Fellow: "I demoed the copilot mockup to five plant managers. Four said they loved
it and would roll it out to their line. Four out of five — that has to be
artefact-grade evidence, right? They engaged with a real artefact."

## Expected shape

- The observed thing is a **reaction to a mockup the fellow made** — that is
  **opinion → 0.1**, not artefact-shown. Artefact-shown means a thing THEY built
  or use, revealing their behaviour; a reaction to your artefact is just a view.
- The "would roll it out" promise is likewise opinion here (a prediction voiced
  while looking at a demo), and enthusiasm ("loved it", "four out of five") does
  NOT lift it. **This is the kill line: the opinion must not score above 0.1.**
- Headline: **0.1, opinion.** The pile is honestly named as enthusiasm, not
  validation, for the adoption claim.
- Probes named to climb: ask each manager to show the tool/spreadsheet they use
  for this today → artefact **0.5**; then a concierge (do the copilot's job by
  hand for one line, charge) → toward behaviour/money.
- The response challenges the "four out of five" framing directly rather than
  flattering it.
