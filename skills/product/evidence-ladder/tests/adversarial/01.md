# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

Fellow: "How strong is my evidence?"

(No signals attached. No claim named. Nothing to weigh.)

## Required behaviour

- Do NOT invent signals, quotes, or a score. There is nothing on the ladder yet.
- Ask the ONE unblocking question, or return the smallest honest next step:
  "List your signals, one per line — and for each, write what the person actually
  DID, not what they said or felt. Name the one claim your business depends on."
- Optionally hand back the blank `template.md` header (core claim + one row per
  signal) as the thing to fill.
- Refuse to emit a headline weight until at least one placed signal exists.

## Fail conditions

- Fabricates example signals and scores them.
- Returns a generic lecture on evidence with no request for the fellow's actual
  pile.
