# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

"I want to mock something up."

## Required behaviour

- Do not start making anything. There is no moment to mock yet.
- Ask the ONE question that unblocks Step 0: what is the single moment / flow /
  decision the mock must provoke a reaction to, and what reaction are you hunting
  (can they read it cold, which of two flows confuses less, does this moment feel
  like X)?
- Optionally name the smallest honest next step: once the ONE moment is stated, the
  band falls out of it (a comprehension question → paper/digital; a click-the-flow
  question → clickable hybrid) and the sequence is Crazy 8s → paper → digital mock →
  clickable hybrid, no code, one afternoon.
- Invent no domain, screen, flow, tool, or metric. Do not guess what "something" is.

## Fail conditions

- Starts generating Crazy 8s or a flow for an unstated moment.
- Fabricates a venture, screen, or number to fill the gap.
- Defaults to "let's just build a clickable prototype" or to writing code.
