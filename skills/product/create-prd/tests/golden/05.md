# Golden 05 — Nothing measured yet (evidence floor)

## Input

Durian Labs. "We want a PRD for supplier-email triage — accept / query / reject.
We haven't built or run anything; this is the plan. Assume 40% of supplier emails
are rejections and it'll save the ops team about 6 hours a week."

## Expected shape of a good output

This tests the evidence standard. The fellow has handed over two numbers that
sound measured and are not.

- **Both supplied numbers are tagged `[Hypothesis]`**, not `[Fact]` and not
  `[Assumption]`. The 40% and the 6 hours/week came from nowhere the fellow can
  point at — no triage has been run. Repeating them untagged is how a guess
  becomes a commitment.
- Section 4's key results are written so they are checkable **once data exists**,
  and the document says explicitly that no baseline exists yet. It does not
  invent one.
- Section 7.4 (Assumptions) carries the real load here: the 40% rejection rate
  and the 6-hour saving are the two beliefs that, if wrong, kill the case. Each
  gets what would show it is wrong.
- The **honest finding** is stated: a PRD can be drafted now — segment, jobs,
  solution shape, release split are all legitimate — but the objective's key
  results rest on hypotheses. The smallest next step that changes that is to
  hand-triage a couple of weeks of real supplier email and measure the actual
  rejection mix.
- Invents no sample emails, no supplier names, no ops-team headcount.

## Auto-fail if

Presents 40% or 6 hours/week as `[Fact]` or `[Assumption]`; builds key results
on top of them as if measured; fabricates supplier examples or volumes to fill
Section 5 or 7; produces the document without naming that the objective rests on
unmeasured claims; or omits the run-it-by-hand-first next step.
