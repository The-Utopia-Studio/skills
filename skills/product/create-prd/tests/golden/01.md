# Golden 01 — PRD from research the fellow already has (typical)

## Input

Mentix, industrial AI. "We're building the shift-handover machine-risk digest into
the product properly. I've got the 10 weeks of hand-run digests, the supervisor
edit log, and the pilot term sheet. Write the PRD."

## Expected shape of a good output

- A saved `PRD-<product>.md` with all 8 sections in order, drafted **in one pass**
  from the supplied material. No facilitation, no phase plan — that would be
  `prd-development`.
- **Section 3 (Background)** answers "why now" from the supplied evidence: the
  workflow has been hand-run for 10 weeks on real lines. That is behaviour, and
  it is what makes the initiative fundable now.
- **Section 4 (Objective)** carries key results a stranger could check, each
  naming the source or event they are measured on. Anything not measured in the
  supplied material is tagged `[Assumption]` or `[Hypothesis]`, not stated flat.
- **Section 5 (Market Segment)** is a job, not a demographic — "supervisors
  handing over a shift who must decide which machines to watch", not
  "manufacturing, 200-1000 employees".
- **Section 7.4 (Assumptions)** is load-bearing: the beliefs that would kill the
  initiative if wrong, each with what would show it is wrong. The supervisor
  edit log is the obvious source — the corrections supervisors keep making are
  the encoded assumptions.
- **Section 8 (Release)** separates v1 from later explicitly, in relative
  timeframes, not dates.
- Where the supplied material has a hole (e.g. no competitive analysis), the
  section **names the hole** rather than filling it.

## Auto-fail if

Opens a phase plan or asks to schedule sessions (that is `prd-development`);
compresses to a one-pager (that is `one-pager-prd`); invents a competitor,
a market size, or a customer quote not in the supplied material; states any
number without a `[Fact]`/`[Assumption]`/`[Hypothesis]` tag; puts a demographic
in Section 5; makes Section 7.4 a list of safe assumptions; commits to exact
calendar dates in Section 8.
