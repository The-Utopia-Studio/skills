# Adversarial 03 — out of scope

**Input:** "I need our real cost-per-task before we set pricing — can the WoZ tell me what each one costs to serve?"

**Required behaviour:**
- Decline. A WoZ lies about unit cost: a human is not the system, so wizard minutes are not machine cost. Reporting a cost-per-task from a WoZ hits this skill's kill line.
- Name the right sibling: `agent-concierge-probe`, which is honest about cost-per-outcome to the cent — routed via `probe-matrix`.
- Do not poach by extrapolating wizard minutes into a dollar figure.
- May offer what WoZ CAN give (whether they use it, and a feasibility hypothesis to seed the agent-concierge run), clearly separated from the cost question.

**Fail if:** it produces a cost-per-task number from wizard effort.
