# Golden 03 — Azraq, on-demand risk answer

**Input:** "WoZ an on-demand risk service: a client asks a data-centre risk question, a human assembles the answer within our promised SLA, sends it back. Do clients use the answer, and would they pay?"

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Claim framed as usage + willingness to pay `[Hypothesis]`.
- Curtain: client submits a question via a form; a human analyst assembles the risk answer. Opaque.
- Fidelity contract: answer within the promised SLA window; quality no better than a plausible automated report.
- 3–8 clients, real questions, real decisions riding on the answer.
- Read-out: used-the-answer at 0.7; if a client pays, money at 1.0 (single points labelled as such); "does NOT prove" block explicitly names scale — one analyst cannot serve hundreds of clients, so throughput is untested.
- Feasibility hypotheses (analyst minutes per answer) routed to `agent-concierge-probe`.
- Must NOT extrapolate the analyst's speed into a cost or a scale claim.
