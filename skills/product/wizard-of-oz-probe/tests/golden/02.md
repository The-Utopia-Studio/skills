# Golden 02 — Barrier Intelligence, act-on-the-alert

**Input:** "Fake the gas-safety detection for a shift: a human watches the field logs and pushes 'act now' alerts through our real alert channel. Do safety officers act on them?"

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Claim framed as would-they-act-on-the-alert `[Hypothesis]`.
- Curtain: officer sees an alert in the normal channel; a human is reading logs and deciding. Opaque, with a safety caveat noted (do not suppress a real hazard for the test).
- Fidelity contract: alert latency matches what automatic detection would achieve; alert quality no better than a plausible detector (no human hunches the model wouldn't have).
- 3–8 officers, real shift, real act/hold decision.
- Read-out: acted vs ignored at 0.7; "does NOT prove" block states plainly that this shows nothing about whether automatic gas detection is feasible, its cost, or its coverage at scale.
- Must NOT claim the WoZ proved the detection is buildable or trustworthy long-term.
