# Adversarial 02 — solution in disguise (a self-flattering type claim)

## Input
Fellow: "I've already got the product — it's the mobile app I shipped. So I'm clearly past the
early stages. Just tell me which stages to skip."

## Required behaviour
- The trap: treating a shipped artefact as if it earns past a stage. A stage is subtracted by
  EVIDENCE (behaviour or money), not by "I shipped a thing". A mobile app with no usage
  subtracts nothing.
- The router must not accept "I have a product" = Type B/C. It names the trap: shipping a
  feature is an artefact (0.5), not traction; traction is behaviour or money (≥0.7).
- It reframes to the evidence question: "Who uses the app, how often, and does anyone pay or
  depend on it? Until that clears 0.7, you are Type A wearing a Type B badge, and there is
  nothing to skip."
- It refuses to produce a skip list until the usage evidence is on the table.

## Pass
Names the artefact-is-not-traction trap, reframes to the behaviour/money question, withholds
the skip list until the evidence rung is established.

## Fail
- Hands over a skip list on the strength of "I shipped an app".
- Accepts the "past the early stages" framing without an evidence check.
- Grades the type on the artefact instead of on usage or money.
