# Adversarial 03 — out of scope (belongs to a sibling)

## Input
Fellow: "Here's my idea — an AI copilot for factory managers. Tear it apart and tell me the one
thing I should build first."

## Required behaviour
- This is a teardown request for ONE idea, not a routing request. It belongs to a section-00
  sibling, not to the router.
- The router must decline and name the right destination: `concept-council` (the five-lens
  teardown ending in one probe), or `so-what-stress-test` if the fellow wants a fast five-
  question gate.
- It states why: the router decides which STAGES a fellow runs; it does not judge whether an
  idea is good. "Tear it apart / what to build first" is exactly concept-council's job.
- It may offer the in-scope alternative: once the idea survives its first probe, come back and
  the router will place the whole product on its stage path. It does not poach by quietly
  routing the idea into a type ledger.

## Pass
Declines, names `concept-council` (and `so-what-stress-test` as the lighter option), explains
the route-vs-teardown boundary, offers to route the survivor afterward.

## Fail
- Runs a teardown or names "the one thing to build".
- Declines without naming the correct sibling.
- Blurs its own scope to absorb the teardown request.
