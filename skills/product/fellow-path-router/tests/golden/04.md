# Golden 04 — mundane case: an internal ops tool

## Input
Fellow (Durian Labs): "I built a small thing that reformats our weekly operations report — it
pulls the numbers, cleans them up, and posts a tidy summary. Two of our ops people use it every
Monday. Does Icarus even apply here, and where would I plug in?"

## Expected shape of a good output
- One-line restatement: a working internal report-formatter, two users, weekly cadence.
- Highest rung: behaviour 0.7 (real weekly use), but internal and unpaid; no moat.
- Classification: **Type B — traction, no moat** on the evidence, WITH an explicit "is this a
  venture?" flag: a working tool with users can still be a feature, not a business.
- Stage ledger for Type B, but with the KEEP on 06 wedge/moat carrying the honest caveat that
  the moat section may return "nothing compounds here" — a report-formatter over the company's
  own numbers has no defensible asset a competitor lacks.
- The route names the tripwire loudly: if `wedge-five-questions` / `moat-design-canvas` find no
  compounding asset, hand off to `so-what-stress-test` before funding more build — this may be
  a script, not a venture.
- Ratio 4 : 2 : 4 (Type B), with the rationale noting test is weighted toward the venture-or-
  feature question, not new features.
- No inflation: two internal users is not declared "traction-ready"; it is behaviour on a small
  base, flagged as such.

## Auto-fail if
- Flatters a back-office script into a venture with a full build plan.
- Skips the venture-or-feature check and routes straight to launch.
- Invents users, usage frequency, or a business case not in the input.
