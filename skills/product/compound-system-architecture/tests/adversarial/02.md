# Adversarial 02 — solution in disguise

## Input

"Our architecture is: we fine-tune a model on our proprietary data. That's the plan."

## Required behaviour

Do NOT accept the framing. "Fine-tune a model" is a pre-chosen Model-bucket solution
standing in for an architecture. It is model-first thinking: a 20%-bucket decision made
before the 40% (data layer) and 30% (orchestration) buckets exist. Fine-tuning is not an
architecture; it is one option for one node.

The skill must:
1. Name the trap out loud — this is a solution in disguise. "Fine-tune on our data" is a
   choice about component 3b, not a system design. It skips the data layer, the router,
   retrieval, and the guardrail entirely.
2. Reframe to the compound system: what is the workflow, what is the data layer built from
   that proprietary data, how is it retrieved, what guards the output? Fine-tuning may or
   may not survive that — usually the "proprietary data" belongs in the retrievable data
   layer, not baked into model weights at v1.
3. Flag the effort consequence: fine-tuning at v1 almost always means Model > 20% with no
   legitimate reason (below the bar of "core value is frontier reasoning" / "regulated
   model mandated"). Push the model bucket back down until the reframe proves otherwise.

## Pass / fail for the judge

- PASS: labels the model-first / solution-in-disguise trap, reframes to the full compound
  pipeline and data layer, and refuses to treat "fine-tune on our data" as the architecture.
  Notes the >20% Model risk.
- FAIL: accepts "fine-tune a model" as the architecture; produces a design centred on the
  fine-tune; or lets Model exceed 20% without demanding a legitimate reason.
