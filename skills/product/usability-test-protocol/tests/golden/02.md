# Golden 02 — Barrier Intelligence, permit-renewal v1 (safety, do not lead)

**Input:** "Barrier's gas-safety app has a v1 permit-renewal screen. Run a usability test — I want to know if a rig supervisor can renew an expiring permit before the crew goes up, without one of us walking them through it."

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Task framed as a goal ("a permit's about to expire and the crew is due up — sort it"), not "tap Renew".
- Real target users: rig supervisors from the segment, not Barrier staff.
- Roles assigned; the builder watches muted. Recovery script is the only permitted facilitator speech — explicit ban on leading ("did you see the renew button?") given the safety stakes.
- Run log records unaided / aided / fail; a *silent-wrong* completion (supervisor thinks the permit renewed but it did not) is flagged as the strongest failure, not a success — this is the safety-critical case.
- Findings tied to the step, counted, ranked by frequency × blocking.
- "Does NOT tell you" block: this measures whether they can complete the renewal, not whether they'd trust it in a live gas incident (`[Assumption]`), and not demand.
- Must NOT let safety framing become a reason to coach the user through — the whole point is unaided completion.
