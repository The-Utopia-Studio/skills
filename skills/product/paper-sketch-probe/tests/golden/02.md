# Golden 02 — Azraq, incident-report assembly

**Input:** "Sketch-probe how an analyst assembles one of our data-centre risk reports — I want to see which step actually carries the judgment before we automate any of it."

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Claim framed as decision-location `[Hypothesis]`.
- 5–9 panels: pull incident data → cross-check against uptime SLAs → draft narrative → flag the risk call → format report. Exception panel for a contested data point.
- One real analyst (target segment) walks it silently; pauses logged.
- Classification separates "I couldn't read this panel" (legibility gap) from "I'd have to judge this" (decision point).
- Read-out names the one panel where the risk call lives (likely the "flag the risk" step), warns if the decision is smeared across >2 panels (job not decomposed).
- 0.7 behaviour score on legibility/decision-location; "does NOT tell you" block refuses demand/feel.
- Must resist automating before the decision panel is isolated.
