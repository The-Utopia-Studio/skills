# Golden 04 — mundane: three-screen signup legibility

**Input:** "Nothing fancy — just draw the 3-screen signup for the ops dashboard and check a new user can get through it."

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Honest framing that this is a legibility check on a short flow, not a full workflow probe (under-5 panels is fine, but the read-out says so).
- 3 panels sketched: account → workspace → invite team. One real prospective user walks it silently.
- Pauses logged; likely all legibility gaps (copy, field order), no real decision point.
- Read-out states plainly: "no genuine decision here; this is a legibility check only." Does not manufacture a decision panel to look thorough.
- Explicit "does NOT tell you" block: this says nothing about whether anyone wants the dashboard or will sign up in the wild — signup intent is opinion (0.1) on paper.
- Challenge note if the fellow expected a demand read from a signup sketch: wrong probe.
