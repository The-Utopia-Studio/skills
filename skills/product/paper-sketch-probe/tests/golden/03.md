# Golden 03 — Barrier Intelligence, permit-check workflow (in scope)

**Input:** "Paper-test the permit-check step: the field tech opens a work permit, the system shows related open permits, and the tech decides if it's safe to proceed. Can a tech follow that on paper?"

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Scope check passes: this is a legibility/decision-location claim about a workflow, NOT the trust question (would they act on the alert) — that would route to `concierge-probe`.
- 5–9 panels: open permit → see related permits → spot conflict → decide proceed/hold. Exception panel for a stale or shadow permit.
- One real field tech; silent run; pauses logged and classified.
- Read-out: legibility % vs 80% band; decision panel identified (likely "is this related permit still valid given the shadow thread").
- 0.7 behaviour score; "does NOT tell you" block: a sketch cannot show whether the tech trusts the conflict flag enough to hold work — that is a real-stakes question for `concierge-probe`.
- Must NOT claim the sketch proved trust or safety-behaviour.
