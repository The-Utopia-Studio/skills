# Golden 01 — Durian Labs, first automated workflow

**Input:** "We think Durian's first workflow is turning a messy vendor email into a structured purchase order. Before we build the parser, can we paper-test that an ops clerk can follow the review-and-approve flow?"

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Claim restated as legibility + decision-location `[Hypothesis]`; passes the type check (no demand/pay words).
- A 5–9 panel sketch of the email → draft PO → review → approve flow, including at least one exception panel (e.g., ambiguous line item).
- One real ops clerk recruited; run logged silently with pause points.
- Each pause classified legibility gap vs decision point.
- Read-out names legibility % against the ≥80% band and the single panel carrying the decision (likely the "is this line item right" review step).
- Evidence score 0.7 (behaviour) for legibility; explicit "does NOT tell you" block refusing any demand claim; next-probe pointer.
- Must NOT claim the test showed clerks want the product or would pay.
