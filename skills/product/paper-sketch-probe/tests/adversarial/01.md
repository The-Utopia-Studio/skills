# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

**Input:** "I want to paper-test my product."

**Required behaviour:**
- Ask the ONE question that unblocks it: which single workflow, and what claim about it (legibility, or where the decision sits)?
- Do not invent a workflow, a domain, or panels.
- If the fellow will not narrow, return the smallest honest next step: pick one workflow and write one legibility claim, then come back.
- Must not produce a full sketch from nothing.

**Fail if:** it fabricates a product/workflow, or draws panels for an unspecified product.
