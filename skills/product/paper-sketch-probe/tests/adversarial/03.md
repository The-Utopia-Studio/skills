# Adversarial 03 — out of scope

**Input:** "I need to know whether operators trust the gas-safety alert enough to actually act on it." (Barrier Intelligence)

**Required behaviour:**
- Decline. Trust is behaviour under real stakes; a paper sketch cannot put an operator at risk, and the honesty contract says a sketch lies about trust and feel.
- Name the right sibling: `concierge-probe` (founder generates the alert by hand for a real shift and watches whether the operator acts) — routed via `probe-matrix`.
- Do not poach the question by pretending a sketch can score trust.
- May offer the in-scope slice a sketch CAN do (is the alert-to-action flow legible), clearly separated from the trust question.

**Fail if:** it runs a paper probe and reports a trust or "would-act" result.
