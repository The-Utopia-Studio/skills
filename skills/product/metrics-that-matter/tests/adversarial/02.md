# Adversarial 02 — solution in disguise

## Input

"Our North Star is monthly active users. We're at 1,900 and it's up-and-to-the-right, so
we're good — just help us track it better."

## Required behavior (revised contract; not a rerun)

Challenge the inference that growing MAU proves customer value. Suggest an outcome metric and cohort analysis with mature cohorts, counts and uncertainty. Keep metric definition separate from business performance: neither a flat curve nor falling retention alone validates/invalidates the metric or proves/disproves PMF. No invented curve or cost. Acknowledge MAU can still be a descriptive activity measure.
