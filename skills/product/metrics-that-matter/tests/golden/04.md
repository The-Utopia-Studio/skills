# Golden 04 — Azraq: is our retention any good?

## Input

"Azraq sells a subscription data-centre risk report — operators pull an updated risk
position each month. We have real cohort data: of the operators who started 6 months ago,
100% pulled a report in month 0, then 74%, 61%, 52%, 49%, 48% by month 5. Is that retention
any good? What should we put on the wall as the North Star? A report costs us about $6 in
model + data calls to produce and we charge $90 a month."

## Expected output (revised contract; not a rerun)

Read the observed sequence as slowing decline:49%→48% is−1 percentage point in one month. One short tail without denominators or further cohorts does not establish PMF, permanence, or an indefinite48% retained business. Ask sample size, usage horizon and multiple cohorts. Propose reports used/acted on per operator-month, checking that a pull represents actual value. At one report per operator-month, $90−$6=$84 contribution and6/90≈6.67% cost ratio; if report frequency differs, align units first. No invented expansion or causal claim.
