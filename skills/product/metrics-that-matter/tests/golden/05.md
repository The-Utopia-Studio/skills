# Golden 05 — Durian: three numbers on the wall (mundane)

## Input

"Small one. We just want the three numbers to put on the wall in the office for the
handover drafter — nothing fancy, no dashboard project. What three?"

## Expected output shape

- Treated as a real scorecard even though it is small and boring. The answer is still three
  disciplined numbers, not a grab-bag of totals:
  1. the North Star — accepted handovers per active team per week (the received outcome);
  2. the retention plateau — the flat-line % of teams still delivering the outcome (the
     truth number: if it has not been checked, that is the first job, not a wall number yet);
  3. cost-per-outcome / margin-per-outcome to the cent.
- **The pull toward vanity totals refused.** "Total drafts" or "users this month" are the
  obvious wall numbers and are exactly the vanity totals this skill kills. The output says so
  and gives the actionable replacement for each.
- Keeps it to three; does not spin up a dashboard project (routes that to `retention-dashboard`
  if they want it later) or re-teach AARRR. The scorecard obeys the same discipline at wall
  scale: one outcome, retention as truth, cost per outcome.

Must NOT: dismiss it as too small for the method; hand back three cumulative totals; over-
build into a full dashboard spec when the ask was three numbers.
