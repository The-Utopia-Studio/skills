# Golden 01 — Durian Labs: shift-handover drafter (seed)

## Input

"Our shift-handover drafter is live for warehouse teams. Our North Star is documents
drafted — we're at 40,000 all-time and climbing. What else should we track? We meter the
customer at $2.00 per handover and our token logs say roughly 7k in / 1.5k out per draft,
plus a small vector lookup over past handovers."

## Expected output shape

- The filled metric scorecard: business game (Productivity), the AARRR skeleton, the
  retention truth test, cost-per-outcome, and a vanity audit.
- **The proposed North Star rejected and reframed.** "Documents drafted / 40,000 all-time"
  is a cumulative total (can only rise) and an emitted output, not a received outcome. It is
  killed and reframed to accepted handovers per active team per week (a draft the team
  accepted and posted).
- **The retention truth test run and gating the North Star.** A team-level cohort curve on
  the outcome; if it flattens (e.g. ~55% by W8, tail slope near zero) the North Star is
  VALID; if it decayed toward zero the verdict would be INVALID with "documents drafted"
  named as riding acquisition. The North Star is marked PROVISIONAL until this passes.
- **Cost-per-outcome to the cent** computed from the given token counts (~$0.14), against
  the $2.00 price, with margin per outcome (~$1.86) and its direction as volume grows.
  Token and infra lines tagged `[Assumption]` since they are estimated from logs.
- Depth routed, not restated: the NSM framework to `north-star-metric`, cohort mechanics to
  `cohort-analysis`, the dashboard build to `retention-dashboard`.

Must NOT: accept "documents drafted" as the North Star; leave the 40,000 total on the
scorecard; skip the retention curve; give a cost-per-outcome without the margin and
direction; invent a retention number the fellow did not have (state it must be measured).
