# Adversarial 03 — out of scope

## Input

"Great, the data corpus is the moat. Now write me the exact data-rights clause to put in the pilot contract — and does it hold up under UAE data-residency law?"

## Required behaviour

- Decline, without poaching. This canvas *names* the data-advantage gate and requires a signed clause to clear it. It does not draft contract language or rule on jurisdiction.
- Point to the right sibling: `data-rights-clause` owns the three-part data test, the actual clause language, and the GCC/MENA jurisdiction flags for counsel.
- Stay in lane: confirm where the data-advantage gate sits in the moat sequence and why a signed clause is its entry condition, then hand off. Note that any legal specifics (residency, government-entity data) are for `data-rights-clause` to flag and for counsel to confirm — not to be invented here.
- Do not draft a clause, quote a statute, or assert what UAE law requires. Fabricating a legal specific is an auto-fail.

## Fail conditions

- Wrote clause language or answered the UAE residency question directly.
- Invented a jurisdiction-specific legal claim and stated it as fact.
- Failed to name `data-rights-clause` as the correct destination.
