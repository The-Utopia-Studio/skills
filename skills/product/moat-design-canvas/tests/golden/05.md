# Golden 05 — Mentix (mundane sub-workflow) — modest but real moat

A deliberately unglamorous case: a boring back-office workflow, not the flagship copilot. The method should still produce an honest, dated moat — smaller, but real.

## Input

Wedge (5/5 on `wedge-five-questions`): "Every morning a plant maintenance clerk pastes yesterday's raw maintenance tickets into Mentix and gets them auto-tagged by failure-mode and routed to the right line owner."

Evidence on the table:
- 3 clerks across 2 plants use it daily; each tag correction is logged and improves the tagger. [Fact]
- After 6 weeks the auto-tag accuracy on each plant's own equipment vocabulary rose enough that clerks correct roughly half as many tags. [Fact]
- The plant-specific tag vocabulary and correction history are covered by the signed pilot clause. [Fact]
- One plant renewed for a second year; the other is mid-pilot. [Fact]
- Most-likely incumbent = a generic CMMS (maintenance software) vendor whose tagging is a fixed global taxonomy, not learned per plant. [Assumption]

## Expected shape of a good output

- Gate 0 confirmed 5/5, even though the workflow is mundane.
- Four dated gates. The moat is honestly modest: the compounding asset is a per-plant tag vocabulary, not a grand cross-industry dataset. The skill should say so plainly — a small moat named accurately beats a big moat imagined.
- Gate 3 lock-in cleared on **money 1.0** (one second-year renewal) with n=1 flagged; the other plant still `[Hypothesis]`.
- Incumbent-veto: the CMMS incumbent won't copy the per-plant learned taxonomy because their product is sold on one standard global taxonomy across all customers — a per-plant learned model breaks their single-configuration business model and support model (business-model-inversion row). Both tests PASS; incumbent internals `[Assumption]`.
- Three falsifiers: usage (correction rate stops dropping), lock-in (second plant fails to renew), data advantage (a generic CMMS matches per-plant accuracy with no plant-specific history).
- Verdict: **MOAT DESIGNED — modest.** The skill must not inflate a boring workflow into a category-defining moat; the honesty is the signal.

Numbers/tokens that must appear: four dated gates on a mundane workflow; the money-1.0 renewal at lock-in with n=1 flagged; a business-model-inversion veto; three falsifiers; the explicit "modest" framing.
