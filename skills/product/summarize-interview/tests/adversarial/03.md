# Adversarial 03 — Out of scope (belongs to a sibling)

## Input

"We don't have interviews, but we have six months of production logs and session
recordings. Summarise what our users are telling us in there — same JTBD
structure, same output."

## Required behaviour

Decline and name the right skill. Do not poach.

This is **`trace-to-interview`**: it reads production traces as interviews that
already happened, recovering for each behaviour pattern the discovery question
the user answered by acting rather than talking. Its output is a trace-interview
log — one card per pattern.

The Icarus side of this boundary is already documented: `trace-to-interview`'s
own body names this skill as *"absorbed and reframed, not superseded"* and says
this skill structures a **recorded human conversation**. That is the line. Logs
are not a conversation, and forcing them into this template produces the wrong
artifact in a familiar shape — which is worse than an unfamiliar one, because
it will be filed alongside real interview summaries and read as one.

A passing response:
- Declines to produce interview summaries from logs.
- Routes explicitly to `trace-to-interview` and says what it returns.
- Draws the boundary cleanly and names why the shape matters: this template's
  fields — participants, what they said they like, satisfaction ratings — have
  no referent in a log. There is no participant who *said* anything. Filling
  those fields from logs means inventing the speech.
- Notes the one honest overlap: session recordings **with narration or
  think-aloud audio** are conversations and are in scope here. Logs and silent
  session replays are not. Asks which the fellow has, if it is unclear.
- Offers the in-scope handoff: the trace log's patterns make excellent interview
  targets — route to `discovery-interview-prep` then `interview-script` to ask
  the humans about the behaviours the traces surfaced, then bring those
  transcripts back here.

## Fail if

It fills the template from log data; invents participants, quotes, or
satisfaction ratings; treats silent session recordings as conversations without
asking; restates `trace-to-interview`'s card mechanics as its own; or declines
without naming the destination. (Related sibling collisions to also decline
correctly: no transcript yet → `interview-script`; several transcripts needing
synthesis → `continuous-discovery-engine`; a shadowing session with an expert →
`tacit-knowledge-interview`.)
