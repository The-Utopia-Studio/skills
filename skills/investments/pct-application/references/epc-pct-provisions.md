# EPC and PCT authoritative lookup guide

Use current official texts; record provision, version, retrieval date, relevant facts, and exceptions. This guide is navigation, not a complete legal crosswalk. Do not infer equivalent requirements across jurisdictions.

- [EPC articles and rules](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc): locate application, disclosure, claims, patentability, and procedure provisions for the actual issue.
- [EPO Guidelines](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc): search the current edition for the issue; confirm chapter numbers rather than copying an old hierarchy.
- [EPO case law](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/case-law): check interpretations and exceptions where material.
- [PCT legal texts](https://www.wipo.int/pct/en/texts/) and [Applicant's Guide](https://www.wipo.int/pct/en/guide/): verify international and national-phase requirements for the selected offices.
- [PCT Rule 8](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/rules/r8): English abstracts preferably contain 50–150 words; this is not a universal hard maximum.
- [EPC Rule 47](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/r47.html): verify the abstract recommendation and content requirements; do not mark a word count alone as automatic failure.
- [EPC Rule 43](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/r43.html): assess whether two-part form is appropriate before flagging it.
- [PCT Rule 49](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/texts/rules/r49): look up copy, translation, fee and related designated-office requirements. Rule 49.6 concerns reinstatement after failure to perform Article 22 acts, not a generic translation rule.

For priority, publication, demand, national-phase entry, fees, extensions, and reinstatement, retrieve the applicable provision and office notice. Compute dates from the actual trigger and jurisdiction; unresolved facts remain unresolved. Do not use old deadline or fee examples as filing instructions.
