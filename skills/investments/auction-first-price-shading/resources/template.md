# Auction analysis template

- Mechanism: first-price buyer auction / reverse procurement / other; reserve, increments, tie rule.
- Value model: independent private / common / mixed, with justification.
- Inputs: value, total N including us, reserve, budget; units and sources.
- Benchmark: formula/model, applicability assumptions and arithmetic.
- Feasible action: abstain or candidate bid; constraints that alter the benchmark.
- Sensitivity: different N/value/reserve or signal assumptions, without changing known inputs.
- Unknowns: what rival, distribution or utility evidence is missing.
- Limitations: no unsupported haircut, no claim of equilibrium beyond the model, no submission.
