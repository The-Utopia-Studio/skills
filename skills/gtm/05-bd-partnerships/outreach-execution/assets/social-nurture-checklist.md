# Social nurture checklist

Merged from social-selling; adapt cadence to useful opportunities rather than activity quotas.

## Daily or session routine
- Review relevant public discussion and community rules.
- Check prior relationship, existing connection, suppression and current drafts before proposing outreach.
- Draft a substantive answer or approved resource offer when it helps the discussion.
- LinkedIn connection request, if appropriate and not already connected: blank, no personalized note.
- Respect DM invitation rules; route live replies to the responsible person.
- Log proposed next steps separately from completed actions.

## Weekly routine
- Review useful contributions, unanswered questions and opted-in follow-ups.
- Update target relevance and remove disqualified or suppressed contacts.
- Review what generated substantive conversations and revise the routine.

## Measures
Track connection acceptance per request, substantive replies per delivered DM, opt-in resource requests, qualified meetings, and relevant interactions per account. Preserve denominators and dates. Set targets from the team's baseline; no universal reply or meeting promise.
