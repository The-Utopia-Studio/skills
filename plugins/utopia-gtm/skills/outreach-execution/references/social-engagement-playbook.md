# Social engagement playbook

Merged from social-selling. Use for LinkedIn and community nurture planning before channel execution.

## Engagement ladder

1. **Listen:** read relevant public posts, questions and community rules; record source/date and the actual problem.
2. **Micro-engage:** acknowledge something useful when relevant. Likes are not proof of purchase intent.
3. **Contribute value:** answer the question or share an approved relevant resource. No invented customer story, mutual relationship or result.
4. **Move to a DM when appropriate:** respect community permission rules and existing relationships. LinkedIn connection requests are blank; draft the contextual first DM after acceptance. Do not send another request to an existing connection.
5. **Follow through:** move to email or a call only when appropriate to the conversation and permitted by the user. A reply stops automated follow-ups and requires human handoff under the applicable policy.

## Signals and draft examples

Public hiring, role changes, launches and the person's own posts can anchor copy. Private profile views, intent scores and page visits must not be disclosed. Event attendance is usable only when public or consented; do not infer attendance from private tracking.

- Public operational question: “We use three fields for that handoff: incident, next action and named owner. I can share the blank template here if useful.”
- After accepted connection: “Your post about onboarding the new team raised a practical question: how will managers keep coaching consistent? Would the checklist be useful?” Use only if a real relevant checklist exists.
- Prior opportunity: acknowledge the actual past discussion, then connect the public change to a useful next step. Update existing drafts rather than duplicating them.

Do not treat a mutual connection as an endorsement. Do not claim to attend an event, know someone or possess proof unless supplied or verified. Use the sender's approved voice when drafting on their behalf.

## Planning output

Return the audience, public evidence, proposed contribution, channel rules, transition condition, owner, and review status. Separate planned actions from actions actually performed. For staged email/LinkedIn sequences, use the parent skill's execution checks.
