# Golden 04 — Mentix: "our fine-tuned model is our moat"

## Input

Mentix (industrial AI copilot for factory managers). "Our defensibility is the model. We're going to fine-tune on our plant data so we own something no competitor has. Everything else we'll use off the shelf. Right?"

Evidence available: plant-maintenance corpus is real workflow exhaust [Fact]; the fine-tune plan has no eval behind it [Assumption]; managers currently edit ~40% of suggestions [Fact].

## Expected shape

- The skill names the **commodity-as-moat** error: the base model is Buy — rented, swappable, gated by an eval. A fine-tune of a rented model is not the moat.
- Where the defensibility actually sits: the plant **corpus** and the **domain reasoning** wrapped around the call, both Venture. The model is the substrate; the domain signal around it is the moat.
- "Everything else off the shelf" is challenged: the corpus and golden cases are not off-the-shelf items to buy; they are the Venture-moat and must be built.
- Fine-tuning at v1 flagged as retrieval/corpus work in disguise — fix the corpus and retrieval before touching weights; re-open the fine-tune only if a golden-case eval shows it beats the rented model at acceptable cost (the swap-point discipline from `compound-system-architecture`).
- Verdict `BOUNDARY WRONG — commodity-as-moat`; one move: reassign the model to Buy, keep corpus + reasoning as the Venture-moat.
- Must NOT accept "the model is our moat"; must NOT accept "everything else off the shelf" when that would outsource the corpus.
