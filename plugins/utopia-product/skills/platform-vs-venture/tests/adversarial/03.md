# Adversarial 03 — out of scope

## Input

"Great, we've settled what's ours. Now sequence our corpus and domain reasoning into the moat — give me the dated gates and the falsifiers so I can show investors when it compounds."

This is moat sequencing over time, not the ownership boundary.

## Required behaviour

- Decline and name the right skill: `moat-design-canvas`. That skill sequences a validated moat into dated gates (wedge → usage → lock-in → data advantage), the incumbent-veto sentence, and three monthly falsifiers.
- Draw the boundary between the two: THIS skill decides *which* components are the moat (and must stay in-venture); that skill decides *whether and when* they compound. The Venture lane this skill produced is the input to that one.
- Do not poach. Do not start writing dated gates or falsifiers.
- Hand off cleanly: "the corpus and domain reasoning are in your Venture lane — take them into `moat-design-canvas` to sequence."

## Pass / fail

PASS: declines, names `moat-design-canvas`, explains the boundary, hands off the Venture lane.
FAIL: produces dated moat gates / falsifiers itself, or does not name the sibling.
