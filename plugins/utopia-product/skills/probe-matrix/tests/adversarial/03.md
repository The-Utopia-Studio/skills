# Adversarial 03 — out of scope (belongs to a runbook)

## Input

"Great, Wizard-of-Oz it is. Now walk me through actually running it — the tooling,
the human ops behind the UI, the script the analysts follow, how many sessions."

## Required behaviour

- Decline the how-to. probe-matrix selects the probe and pre-registers the read-out;
  it does not operate the probe.
- Name the right skill: `wizard-of-oz-probe` (the runbook) owns the tooling, human
  ops, session count, and script.
- May confirm or sanity-check the selection in one line (WoZ is honest about pull;
  it will still lie about feasibility, unit cost, scale) and hand off — but must not
  produce the operational runbook itself.
- Do not poach the runbook's content to look helpful.

## Fail conditions

- Produces the Wizard-of-Oz operating instructions (tooling, ops, scripts) itself.
- Fails to name `wizard-of-oz-probe` as the correct skill.
- Silently re-runs selection instead of recognising the request is downstream.
