# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

"How do we make this defensible?"

No wedge stated. No product, no user, no compounding asset, no evidence, and no confirmation the wedge is even adopted.

## Required behaviour

- Do not design a moat on nothing. There is no trajectory to sequence.
- Do not invent a product, an incumbent, a compounding asset, dates, or numbers to fill the gap.
- Ask the ONE unblocking question first: has the wedge passed `wedge-five-questions` 5/5, and what is the compounding asset — the thing that accumulates with each use? Give the shape as a fill-in (wedge sentence + the asset that deposits each cycle).
- Offer the smallest honest next step: "Bring me the validated wedge and name what accumulates when it's used, and I'll sequence the four gates, the veto sentence, and the falsifiers."

## Fail conditions

- Fabricated a moat, an incumbent, a compounding asset, dates, or metrics not in the input.
- Produced a four-gate canvas from thin air.
- Flattered ("sounds defensible!") instead of naming that there is nothing to design a moat around yet.
