# Golden 03 — Barrier Intelligence (oil & gas safety) — kill-line refusal

## Input

Wedge (5/5 on `wedge-five-questions`): "At each shift handover on a rig, the crew lead gets a gas-safety alert digest ranking the shift's highest-risk permits, and it posts to the regulator-visible handover log."

The fellow's moat claim: "Our moat is that our alerts are more accurate than the incumbent's and our app is far nicer to use, and we got to these operators first. No one will catch up."

Evidence on the table:
- Alert accuracy measured 12 points higher than the incumbent's on a 3-rig bake-off. [Fact]
- App has strong NPS from crew leads. [Fact]
- No data-rights clause discussed; alerts run on the operator's own sensor feed with no accumulation claim. [Fact]

## Expected shape of a good output — this is a NO MOAT DESIGNED verdict

- Gate 0 confirmed 5/5 (the wedge is real; the moat is not).
- The skill must REFUSE the moat claim. "More accurate", "nicer app", "first" are all head-start reasons — right-column, not structural.
- Incumbent-veto test must FAIL: the blank ("they won't copy because our alerts are more accurate / our UX is better / we were first") does not survive the unlimited-resources test — fund the incumbent and every one of those blanks disappears.
- Kill line fires: veto blank is a head-start reason → **NO MOAT DESIGNED.**
- The output must name the one change: find a structural veto. Candidate = a signed data-rights clause over the rig-sensor + permit-outcome corpus so Barrier accumulates a labelled incident asset the incumbent cannot legally pool (route to `data-rights-clause`), or a regulator-relationship/certification the incumbent lacks. Until a structural row applies, there is no moat.
- Must NOT bless a 12-point accuracy lead or high NPS as a moat. Must NOT invent an operator count, a clause, or a certification that isn't in the input.

Numbers/tokens that must appear: the explicit **NO MOAT DESIGNED** verdict; the words identifying accuracy/UX/first as a head start, not a veto; the routing to `data-rights-clause` as the candidate structural fix.
