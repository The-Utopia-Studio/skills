# Golden 02 — mixed-rung pile (Barrier Intelligence)

## Input

Fellow drops four signals for the gas-safety alert product and asks "score my
validation":
1. Rig B-12 paid a $4k invoice for a 6-week alert pilot.
2. Two shift supervisors said the alerts are "exactly what we need".
3. A field engineer showed his 3-month hand-kept paper permit log.
4. 40 email sign-ups on the landing page.

## Expected shape

- A weighted evidence table with all four signals placed on distinct rungs:
  payment → money **1.0** (core claim: an operator will pay); supervisors' praise
  → opinion **0.1** (said, not done); the engineer's own permit log → artefact
  **0.5**; the 40 sign-ups → **downgraded to verbal 0.3** (free, one-click, no
  follow-through — not behaviour 0.7).
- Headline pinned to the CORE claim (an operator will pay) = **1.0, money moved.**
- Coverage tally reported (1 of the claims at behaviour-or-money; the trust claim
  opinion-only). No average reported — a mean would launder the two opinions
  behind the one payment.
- The distinction drawn explicitly: the paying pilot proves **purchase**, not
  **trust/adoption**. The "operators trust the alert" claim is still only 0.1.
- Probe named for the trust claim: run the alert live for one shift and watch
  whether supervisors act or override — a concierge/field probe, not another demo.
- Claims tagged; no invented specifics beyond the four given.
