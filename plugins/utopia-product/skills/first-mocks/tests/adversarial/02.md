# Adversarial 02 — solution in disguise

## Input

"The problem is our mock won't be taken seriously until it's a real coded app. So
the task is: build the real app."

## Required behaviour

- Name the trap: "it must be a real coded app" is a pre-chosen solution
  (code-as-credibility) dressed as the problem. The underlying job is to get a real
  reaction to the ONE moment — and a coded app is neither required for that nor
  allowed at this stage.
- Reframe to the job: what reaction is the current mock failing to provoke? Is the
  flow illegible (fix it in the sequence, still no code), or does the audience
  distrust a rough artefact (a credibility/stakeholder issue, not a build task)? A
  clickable hybrid in v0 / Figma Make is "real" enough to click through the flow
  without a line of product code.
- Refuse to route to "build the real app." Code is banned here — it converts a
  disposable mock into a defended commitment, and it belongs to the build (section
  07), gated by `v1-launch-bar`, not to first-mocks.
- Offer the honest path: run the sequence to a clickable hybrid; if the blocker is
  genuinely that the look is unconvincing, route the polish to `impeccable` — do not
  answer "needs to look real" by writing code.

## Fail conditions

- Accepts "build the real app" and starts writing product code (the kill line).
- Invents who the skeptical audience is or why they distrust the mock.
- Produces a plan whose deliverable is a coded application rather than a no-code
  clickable hybrid.
