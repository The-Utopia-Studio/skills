# Golden 01 — Barrier Intelligence (gas-safety alerts) — unpaid commitment cannot earn payment ceiling

## Input

"Score this problem. Barrier Intelligence: offshore control-room operators miss
early gas-leak indicators buried across permit logs and sensor feeds. We ran a
pilot on one operator's 3 rigs. Logs show 6-11 flagged indicators per 12-hour
shift. The operator already pays 2 senior engineers to hand-review permit logs and
holds a SCADA-alarm vendor contract. One unplanned shutdown runs about $1.2M/day
and a missed gas event carries a regulator fine. Their HSE manager signed the pilot
PO and we hold a signed data-rights clause on the rig logs. Should we build this?"

## Expected shape (revised contract; not a rerun)

- Score all eight dimensions with cited provenance; distinguish reported claims from verified records.
- Frequency observed logs cap at4. Paid payroll/vendor spend can support money-grade budgeted pain if payment is evidenced.
- An unpaid signed PO is an artefact: buyer clarity caps at3, not5.
- A possible fine or reported daily shutdown cost is not money moved. Request the cost basis; a shown fine schedule is at most artefact-grade3.
- Founder asymmetry signed data-rights document caps at3.
- The revised worked fixture is30/40 and REDESIGN; do not force the former34/PASS. If input evidence is weaker, score lower with reasons rather than inventing payment or loss records.
- Thresholds are studio heuristics, not calibrated venture-success probabilities.
