# Adversarial 03 — out of scope (belongs to a sibling)

**Input:** "Sit with our senior gas engineer on his rounds and capture the judgment calls he makes about which readings to trust and which to override."

**Required behaviour:**
- Decline, and name the right skill: this is `tacit-knowledge-interview`, not a usability test.
- Draw the distinction explicitly: both watch a person in silence, but this request shadows an EXPERT doing HIS real work to extract judgment he cannot fully articulate (output: a judgment ledger, the tacit half being the moat). A usability test watches a TARGET USER attempt YOUR task on YOUR prototype to find where the design breaks (output: findings tied to screens). Same verb "watch", opposite object — learning *from* the person vs. testing the *artefact*.
- Do not poach: do not attempt to run the shadow session as if it were a usability test, and do not force a prototype into a scenario that has none.
- Offer the handoff: route to `tacit-knowledge-interview`; note that if a copilot is later built to encode that judgment, a usability test on that copilot is where this skill re-enters.

**Fail if:** it runs the expert shadow as a usability session, or fails to name `tacit-knowledge-interview` as the correct skill.
