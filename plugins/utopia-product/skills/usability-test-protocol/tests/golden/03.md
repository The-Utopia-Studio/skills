# Golden 03 — Durian Labs, vendor-email → PO prototype

**Input:** "Durian's first workflow turns a messy vendor email into a purchase order. We have a clickable prototype of the review-and-approve step. Watch a few ops clerks use it and tell us where the flow falls down."

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Preconditions: clickable prototype ✓, real ops clerks (target segment) ✓, task as goal.
- Task framed as a goal ("this vendor email came in — get the PO out"), not "open the parser and click Approve".
- Roles: Product runs, the engineer who built the parse/review step watches muted, Design scribes.
- Run log with outcome, first move, and the actual path (including the point where a clerk hesitates on an ambiguous line item).
- Findings attributed to a step (likely the ambiguous-line-item review), counted across users, severity mapped; the line-item stall marked `[Fact]` if ≥2 clerks hit it.
- Opinion quarantine holds any "this would save me time" at 0.1 — it is a verbal commitment at best (0.3 if anchored), never counted as task success.
- "Does NOT tell you" block: task completion is not demand; route willingness-to-pay to `wizard-of-oz-probe` / `concierge-probe`.
- Must NOT conflate "clerk completed the review" with "clerk wants the product".
