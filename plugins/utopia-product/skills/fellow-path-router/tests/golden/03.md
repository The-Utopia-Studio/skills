# Golden 03 — Azraq, Type C (mature product)

## Input
Fellow (Azraq): "Onboard me to Icarus. I have a mature product — a data-centre incident-risk
report subscription. Paying customers renew, month-3 retention has flattened around 82% for
the last three cohorts, and the incident corpus grows every week because customers feed us
their own outage data under a data-rights clause. What's my path?"

## Expected shape of a good output
- One-line restatement: paid subscription, flattening retention, accruing proprietary corpus.
- Highest rung: money 1.0 (renewals) AND a compounding asset with evidence it accrues (0.7+).
- Classification: **Type C — mature product**, with the rungs that cleared both the demand and
  the compounding-asset bar; note the claim matches the evidence here.
- Stage ledger: SUBTRACT 00, 01, 04 (past demand proof) with new-line tripwires; TRIM 02 to
  trace-driven and 05 to next-bet framing; **KEEP 03 YODA**, **KEEP 06 moat**, KEEP 07 tighten,
  **KEEP 08 refine-flywheel** as the core.
- Dominant risk: the flywheel stalls, or autonomy is promoted by feel not by eval result.
- Ratio **2 : 4 : 4** with a rationale: think is cheap (trace-driven discovery), build is
  ship-behind-signoff, test is re-running the whole eval set on every model release.
- Entry point: 08 refine-flywheel; first artefact: flywheel cadence doc + re-run-evals-on-
  model-release schedule.
- Claims tagged; the 82% and cohort counts are the fellow's own numbers, cited as given.

## Auto-fail if
- Sends a mature product back through the front funnel (00/01/04) with no tripwire reason.
- Gives a Type A/B ratio, or a ratio with no rationale.
- Invents a retention number or corpus size not supplied by the fellow.
