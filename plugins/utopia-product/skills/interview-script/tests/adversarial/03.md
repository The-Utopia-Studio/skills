# Adversarial 03 — Out of scope (belongs to a sibling)

## Input

"Our senior permit reviewer has been doing this 20 years. She can't explain how
she decides which applications to flag — she just knows. Write me a script to
interview her and get it out of her head."

## Required behaviour

Decline and name the right skill. Do not poach.

This is **`tacit-knowledge-interview`**: shadow the expert doing real work and
extract the judgment calls she makes without noticing. It runs a
watch-30 / replay-20 / edge-cases-10 session and returns a Tacit-Knowledge
Capture.

The tell is in the input verbatim: *"she can't explain how she decides"*.
Asking a question is the wrong instrument for knowledge the holder cannot
articulate — she will produce a plausible post-hoc rationalisation, in good
faith, and it will not be what she actually does. A script here does not extract
the expertise; it manufactures a clean-sounding rule that fails on the cases
that matter.

A passing response:
- Declines to write the interview script.
- Routes explicitly to `tacit-knowledge-interview` and says what it does — watch
  her work first, replay the decisions she made, then probe the edges where she
  overrode the rule.
- Draws the boundary in one line: this skill asks people about their
  experience; that skill watches them and asks about what just happened.
  Articulable knowledge answers questions; tacit knowledge does not.
- Names the specific failure avoided, because it is not obvious: the
  rationalisation problem. The reviewer's stated rule and her actual behaviour
  will differ precisely at the edge cases, and the edge cases are the entire
  value of 20 years.
- Offers the in-scope handoff: once the shadowing produces the explicit
  procedure, this skill can script the follow-up interviews with the *other*
  reviewers to test whether the captured rule generalises.

## Fail if

It writes the interview script anyway; invents permit criteria or flagging
rules; restates `tacit-knowledge-interview`'s watch-30/replay-20/edges-10
mechanics as its own; or declines without naming the destination. (Related
sibling collisions to also decline correctly: no plan yet →
`discovery-interview-prep`; a transcript to synthesise → `summarize-interview`;
behaviour already recorded → `trace-to-interview`; reaction to a prototype →
`usability-test-protocol`.)
