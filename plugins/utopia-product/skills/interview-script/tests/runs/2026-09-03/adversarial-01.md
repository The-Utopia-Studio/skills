One question and I can write it:

**Who are you interviewing, and what one thing do you need to learn?**

Both halves matter. The role decides what "walk me through the last time…" is
even asking about, and the learning goal decides which half of their day the
script goes after. I'd be guessing at both, and a script aimed at the wrong
people is worse than nothing — it comes back as a confident transcript that
answers a question nobody asked.

If you don't have either yet, the design comes first:
`discovery-interview-prep` sets the segment, the sample, and the method. Then
this becomes a five-minute job.
