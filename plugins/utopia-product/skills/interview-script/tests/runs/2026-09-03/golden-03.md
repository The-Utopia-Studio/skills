I can write this, but not yet — and the missing piece isn't the questions.

"Our product" isn't a research target. A script is a set of questions aimed at
specific people about a specific decision, and I'd be inventing both. A script
without a research design behind it is a list of good questions pointed at the
wrong people, and you'd find that out on the calls rather than now.

**Two facts unblock me:**

1. **Who are you talking to?** Not a persona — an actual role at an actual kind
   of company, the people whose calls are booked or bookable.
2. **What one thing do you need to learn?** Phrased so it could come back
   "no". "Learn about our users" can't.

Give me those two and I'll write the script. They don't need to be a formal
document — they need to exist.

If you *don't* have them, that's the real gap and it comes first:
`discovery-interview-prep` decides who, how many, and by what method. The order
isn't reversible — a script built on the wrong segment is worse than no script,
because it produces a confident transcript that answers nothing.
