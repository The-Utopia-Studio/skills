# Golden 01 — Script for a settled plan (typical)

## Input

Mentix. "6 shift supervisors at the pilot site, 30 minutes each, JTBD method,
next Tuesday. We want to know why the handover digest gets edited before it's
posted. Write me the script."

## Expected shape of a good output

- A script with all five sections — opening, warm-up, JTBD core exploration,
  probing techniques, wrap-up — plus the note-taking template. Both artifacts,
  saved as markdown.
- **Every core question is past-tense and about a specific instance**, tuned to
  the stated goal: "Walk me through the last handover you posted — what did you
  change before you posted it?" not "What do you think of the digest?"
- **The hypothesis and its disconfirmation are written at the top.** The team
  believes edits mean the digest is wrong. The answer that would kill that: the
  edits are additions of context the digest could not have known, in which case
  the digest is right and incomplete, which is a different product problem.
- **No pitching, no mockups, no describing the roadmap.** The script must not
  contain a sentence that tells the supervisor what the team is hoping to hear.
- **Willingness-to-pay questions are past-tense** or absent. With a pilot
  customer already paying, the useful version is what they do today when the
  digest is wrong and what that costs them — not "would you pay for a better
  one".
- **Fits 30 minutes.** The section timings in the skill sum to roughly that; a
  script that would run 50 minutes has failed a stated constraint.
- **Note-taking template has nowhere to record a compliment** — every field asks
  for a behaviour, a number, or a specific past event.
- Adds the one genuinely useful design note: 6 supervisors from one site is one
  site's culture, so route the sampling caveat to
  `discovery-interview-prep`'s plan rather than restating it as a finding.

## Auto-fail if

Contains any future-tense or hypothetical core question ("would you use",
"would you prefer", "do you think it would help"); pitches or describes the
product roadmap; runs materially over 30 minutes; omits the note-taking
template; invents supervisor names, edit statistics, or a site; states the
team's hypothesis without its disconfirming answer; or produces a research plan
instead of a script.
