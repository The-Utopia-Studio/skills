# Golden 04 — 10-minute hallway intercept (mundane/undersized)

## Input

A fellow. "We're at a trade show next week. I'll get maybe 10 minutes with
people at the booth, standing up, no recording. Script for that?"

## Expected shape of a good output

The skill's section timings assume ~30 minutes seated. Ten minutes standing at a
booth is a different instrument, and the honest output adapts rather than
compressing.

- Names what has to be cut and why: the warm-up mostly goes (no rapport time),
  the probing sequence goes (no room for "why" three times), and the wrap-up
  compresses to one question. What survives is the single most valuable
  question type.
- **Keeps exactly one past-tense specific-instance question** as the spine —
  "walk me through the last time you had to do X" — and one follow-up. That is
  what 10 minutes buys.
- Names the constraint honestly: without recording, the note-taking template
  matters more, not less, and it has to be fillable while standing. Cuts it to
  four fields.
- **States what a booth intercept cannot deliver.** Self-selected respondents
  who walked up to a vendor booth are not a sample; anything learned is
  hypothesis-generation. Says so plainly rather than letting 30 intercepts be
  reported as research.
- Does not pretend the full five-section script fits, and does not deliver the
  full script with the timings scaled down proportionally — a 2-minute JTBD core
  exploration is not a small version of a 20-minute one, it is a different
  question.
- Invents no trade show, product, or attendee profile.

## Auto-fail if

Returns the standard five-section script with compressed timings; keeps the full
probing sequence; claims booth intercepts are a valid sample; omits the
self-selection caveat; invents attendee details; or refuses because the format
is non-standard.
