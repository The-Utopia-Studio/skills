# Approved words — ASD-STE100 Issue 9 dictionary

Every word below is **approved** in Simplified Technical English, and only as the part of
speech shown (Rule 1.2) and with the approved meaning given in the standard (Rule 1.3).
Technical nouns and technical verbs are NOT in this list — they are approved by category
instead (see `writing-rules.md`, rules 1.5 and 1.12).

A word that is not here and is not a technical noun or technical verb is **not permitted**.
Look it up in `replacements.md` for the approved alternative.
