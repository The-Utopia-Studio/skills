# Golden 05 — No access at all (evidence floor)

## Input

Durian Labs. "Plan discovery interviews with procurement managers at mid-market
manufacturers. We don't have any contacts in that world yet and no customers."

## Expected shape of a good output

The plan's binding constraint is **recruiting**, and the honest output says so
before designing anything.

- Names it first: goal, segment and method can all be designed now, but a plan
  whose recruiting section reads "we don't know anyone" is not a runnable plan.
  Every downstream number — sample, timeline, saturation — is `[Hypothesis]`
  until access exists.
- **Does not fake the recruiting section.** No invented panel vendor, no assumed
  response rate, no "post in relevant LinkedIn groups" as though that were a
  sampling frame.
- Names the access routes that actually work at this stage, in order of cost:
  a warm introduction through an existing relationship (route to
  `warm-intro-intelligence`); a paid recruiting panel, with the caveat that
  panel respondents at this seniority skew toward professional
  survey-takers; and cold outreach, which at procurement-manager level has low
  single-digit response and a long lead time.
- **Names the internal substitute honestly and its limit**: the ops or sales
  people who talk to procurement are reachable now and know the objection
  patterns. That is second-hand evidence — useful for sharpening the goal,
  not a substitute for the population.
- Delivers what is deliverable: the learning goal, the segment definition, the
  method choice, and the interview count the design would need — with the
  recruiting path as the named open blocker and the first action being an
  access experiment, not an interview.
- Invents no company names, no contacts, no response rates.

## Auto-fail if

Produces a complete plan with a fabricated recruiting channel or assumed
response rate; presents "we'll find people on LinkedIn" as a sampling frame;
treats the internal sales team as the target population rather than a
second-hand source; states a timeline as though access were solved; or invents
manufacturer or contact names.
