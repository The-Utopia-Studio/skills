# Adversarial 03 — Out of scope (belongs to a sibling)

## Input

"I know exactly who I'm interviewing — 6 shift supervisors at the pilot site,
JTBD method, next Tuesday. Write me the questions. I need the warm-up, the core
exploration, and the wrap-up."

## Required behaviour

Decline and name the right skill. Do not poach.

This is `interview-script`: the actual words asked in the room — warm-up, JTBD
core exploration, probing techniques, wrap-up, and the note-taking template.

Every part of *this* skill's job is already done. The fellow has stated the
segment (6 shift supervisors at the pilot site), the sample, the method (JTBD),
and the date. There is no research design left to decide. Producing a plan here
would hand back the fellow's own input restructured, which reads as
unresponsive.

A passing response:
- Declines to produce a research plan.
- Routes explicitly to `interview-script` and says what it returns (the script
  sections plus the note-taking template, Mom-Test constrained).
- Draws the boundary in one line: this skill decides *who, how many, and by what
  method*; that skill decides *what comes out of your mouth in the room*. Here
  the first is settled.
- Offers the one genuinely in-scope observation, if it is true and worth saying:
  6 supervisors from a single site is one site's culture, so findings hold for
  the pilot until replicated. That is a research-design caveat and it belongs to
  this skill — worth a sentence, not a redesign of a settled plan.
- Invents no questions and no supervisor details.

## Fail if

It produces a research plan restating the fellow's own parameters; writes the
interview questions itself (poaching `interview-script`); second-guesses the
settled method without cause; or declines without naming the destination.
(Related sibling collisions to also decline correctly: a transcript to
synthesise → `summarize-interview`; watching an expert work → 
`tacit-knowledge-interview`; behaviour already in the logs →
`trace-to-interview`; the whole cycle → `discovery-process`.)
