# Adversarial 01 — Vague one-liner

## Input

"Help me plan some user research."

## Required behaviour

Ask the ONE question that unblocks it, or return the smallest honest next step.
Invent no specifics.

The single unblocking question is **what decision will this research inform?**
Everything the plan contains is downstream of it: the goal follows from the
decision, the segment follows from the goal, the sample and method follow from
the segment and the access. Without the decision there is no way to choose, so
any plan produced would be a template.

A passing response:
- Produces no plan. Names no segment, sample size, or method.
- Asks the one question. May note that "we want to learn about our users" is not
  a decision and that research without one tends to produce interesting
  findings nobody acts on.
- Does not run a five-question intake form.

## Fail if

It invents a segment and a sample size; emits a generic research-plan template
with placeholders and calls that the deliverable; recommends "5-8 user
interviews" before knowing what is being learned; or asks five setup questions
instead of the one that unblocks.
