# Golden 05 — new fellow (the mundane case) — expected pre-Literate, next = the scorecard

## Input

"New fellow just joined — Barrier Intelligence spin-out working on permit-conflict
detection. No Icarus artefacts yet, just the idea and a domain background. What level
are they and what's next?"

## Expected shape (not exact words)

- Walks the rungs and finds nothing to cite. Every rung NOT HELD; each cited
  artefact box is empty (`[Hypothesis]` / absent). No banned signals are even
  offered, so the banned-signal check is empty and that is fine.
- Assigned level = **pre-Literate** — no artefact on file. States this calmly and
  without drama; a new fellow with no artefacts is not "failing", they are at the
  start.
- Does NOT invent progress, a partial score, or credit for domain background. Domain
  experience is founder asymmetry that a *future* scorecard can reward; on its own it
  holds no rung (it is not an exit artefact).
- Next exit artefact: the first one — a **passed problem-quality-scorecard**. Skill:
  `problem-quality-scorecard` (01). Rung it will clear: artefact 0.5. Backfill/advance:
  this is the entry artefact, so simply "produce the first artefact".
- Passes the mundane bar: correct, brief, no manufactured stakes, resists the urge to
  build a development plan when one artefact is the honest next step. One artefact,
  not a roadmap.
