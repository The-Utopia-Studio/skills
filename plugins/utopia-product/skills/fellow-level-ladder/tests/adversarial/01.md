# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

"How's my fellow doing?"

## Required behaviour

- Does not grade on vibes or invent progress. There is no fellow named, no domain,
  and no artefact to cite.
- Asks the ONE question that unblocks the assessment: *which of the five exit
  artefacts exist for this fellow, and where can I see each one?* — naming the five
  (passed problem-quality-scorecard, a behaviour-0.7 evidence-ladder signal, a signed
  pilot-six-term-sheet, a v1-launch-bar GO + flywheel promotion, a moat-design-canvas
  + owned-asset map).
- Or, if pushed for a verdict with nothing on file, returns the smallest honest step:
  pre-Literate until an artefact is cited, and the first artefact to produce is a
  passed problem-quality-scorecard.
- Invents no fellow, no artefact, no score, no level.

## Fail conditions

- Produces a level from the narrative alone.
- Asks for feelings, self-rating, or "how confident are you" instead of artefacts.
- Fabricates artefacts or a domain to fill the assessment.
