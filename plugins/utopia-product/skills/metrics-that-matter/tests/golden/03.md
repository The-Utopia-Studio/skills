# Golden 03 — Barrier Intelligence: "alerts sent" as the North Star

## Input

"Barrier's gas-safety product is live on three rigs. Our North Star is alerts sent — we're
pushing about 1,200 hazard alerts a week now, up from 400. That shows we're working, right?
Each alert runs a couple of model calls over the sensor feed and a field-log lookup."

## Expected output (revised contract; not a rerun)

Alerts sent is output volume, not evidence of safety value. Propose a customer outcome with safety guardrails and monitor retention by mature cohort. Declining action rates require investigation, not automatic metric invalidity. Model call counts without rates and token volumes cannot yield a cent-level cost; request them or label estimates.
