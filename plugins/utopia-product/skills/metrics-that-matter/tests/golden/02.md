# Golden 02 — Mentix: MAU up-and-to-the-right, is it real?

## Input

"Mentix's factory copilot MAU is up-and-to-the-right — 300 → 1,900 monthly active managers
over two quarters, and we've been buying pilots hard. The board loves the chart. Is this
real? What should our actual North Star be? We've never cut it by cohort."

## Expected output (revised contract; not a rerun)

MAU growth alone cannot distinguish retained value from acquisition. Propose an outcome metric and request cohort counts, ages, instrumentation and uncertainty. A decline is poor performance, not automatic invalidity of the metric; a flat tail alone does not prove PMF. No invented retention or costs.
