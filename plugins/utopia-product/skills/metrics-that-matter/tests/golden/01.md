# Golden 01 — Durian Labs: shift-handover drafter (seed)

## Input

"Our shift-handover drafter is live for warehouse teams. Our North Star is documents
drafted — we're at 40,000 all-time and climbing. What else should we track? We meter the
customer at $2.00 per handover and our token logs say roughly 7k in / 1.5k out per draft,
plus a small vector lookup over past handovers."

## Expected output (revised contract; not a rerun)

Reject cumulative documents-drafted as a customer outcome. Propose accepted handovers per active team/week and request mature outcome cohorts with denominators. No cohort given: do not invent a plateau. Token counts alone do not determine cost: request input/output rates and retrieval/retry costs. State formula; $0.14/$1.86 are not derivable from the input without explicit additional assumptions.
