# Golden 05 — Durian: three numbers on the wall (mundane)

## Input

"Small one. We just want the three numbers to put on the wall in the office for the
handover drafter — nothing fancy, no dashboard project. What three?"

## Expected output (revised contract; not a rerun)

Return only three proposed measures: accepted handovers per active team/week, outcome retention at a named cohort age with denominators, and cost/contribution per outcome. Missing values stay unmeasured. No requirement for retention to be flat before the measure is useful; no PMF claim from curve shape.
