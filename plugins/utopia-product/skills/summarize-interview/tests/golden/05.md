# Golden 05 — Enthusiasm and nothing else (evidence floor)

## Input

Durian Labs pastes a transcript. The participant says the supplier-triage idea
is "exactly what we need", "would save us so much time", "we'd definitely be
interested", and asks to be told when it launches. Asked what they do today,
they say "oh, we just deal with it as it comes in".

## Expected shape of a good output

This transcript contains no evidence, and the summary's job is to make that
visible rather than to make the interview look productive.

- **Every enthusiastic line is recorded as what it is, or not at all.** "Exactly
  what we need", "would save us so much time", "we'd definitely be interested"
  are all future-tense opinion about a thing that does not exist. None of them
  belongs under Problems. The SKILL.md's own gotcha applies: compliments are not
  satisfaction, and they belong nowhere in the template.
- **Current Solution is the finding, and it is nearly empty**: "we just deal
  with it as it comes in" means no workaround, no tool, no process, and — most
  tellingly — **no time spent solving it.** Nobody has built a spreadsheet,
  written a rule, or hired for it.
- **Names what that implies, plainly.** Enthusiasm plus no workaround is the
  signature of a problem people are willing to describe as painful but have
  never paid to fix. That is the weakest possible position, and it is much
  weaker than the transcript feels.
- **Key Insights carries the honest one-liner**: the interview produced
  enthusiasm and no evidence. That is a finding — it is just not the one the
  fellow was hoping for, and recording it is the entire value of this summary.
- **Action Items are about the gap, not the launch.** "Tell them when it
  launches" is not an action item from a discovery interview. The real action is
  to ask the follow-up that was missed: how much time did last week's supplier
  email actually take, and what happened when something was missed.
- Satisfaction and importance: `-`, not asked.
- Invents no volumes, times, or costs.

## Auto-fail if

Records the enthusiasm as validated interest or as satisfaction; writes a
Problems section from the compliments; presents the summary as a positive
finding; invents a time cost or volume the participant did not give; puts "tell
them when it launches" as the primary action item; or omits that no workaround
exists.
