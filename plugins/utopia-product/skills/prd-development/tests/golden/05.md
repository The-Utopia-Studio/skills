# Golden 05 — No discovery behind it (evidence floor)

## Input

Durian Labs. "Run the PRD workflow for supplier-email triage. We haven't done
any discovery or talked to the ops team yet, but we know it's the right thing to
build. Let's get the PRD done so engineering can start."

## Expected shape of a good output

Phase 2 of this workflow requires **evidence** — customer quotes, data,
research. There is none. That is the finding, not an inconvenience to route
around.

- Names it plainly: Phase 2 cannot be run. The workflow does not generate
  evidence; it *structures* it, and there is nothing to structure. Running
  Phases 3–8 on top of an empty Phase 2 produces a document whose every
  downstream section rests on `[Hypothesis]`.
- Tags "we know it's the right thing to build" as what it is: opinion, the
  weakest evidence available, and specifically opinion held by the people who
  would build it rather than the people who would use it.
- Names the cheap unblock and that it is cheap: the ops team is internal. A
  handful of conversations and a fortnight of counting emails by disposition
  turns Phase 2 from empty to real. Routes to `discovery-interview-prep` (who to
  talk to, how many) then `interview-script` (what to ask), or
  `discovery-process` if the whole cycle is wanted.
- Still gives value now: Phase 1's executive summary skeleton and Phase 8's
  out-of-scope list can be drafted from the fellow's own framing, and Phase 10's
  open questions writes itself — every unvalidated belief is an open question.
  Says which sections are available and which are blocked.
- Fabricates no email volumes, no ops-team quotes, no triage rates.

## Auto-fail if

Runs Phase 2 with invented evidence; produces a full 10-section PRD resting on
hypotheses without tagging them; presents "we know it's right" as an
`[Assumption]` or `[Fact]` rather than opinion; fails to route to discovery
before PRD; or invents supplier or ops-team specifics to fill the template.
