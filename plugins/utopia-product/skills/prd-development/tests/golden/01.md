# Golden 01 — Facilitated PRD build after discovery (typical)

## Input

Mentix. "We finished the discovery cycle on shift handover. I've got interview
notes from 9 supervisors, the retention curve, and a rough solution sketch, but
nothing written down properly and no alignment. Design, engineering and I have
three afternoons next week. Run us through building the PRD."

## Expected shape of a good output

- Opens as a **facilitated workflow**, not a document generator: names the 8
  phases, maps them onto the three afternoons, and states the total (2-4 days of
  session time) up front so the fellow can confirm the commitment.
- Adopts `workshop-facilitation` as the interaction protocol — one question per
  turn, progress labels, numbered options, pause/resume — rather than emitting
  all 8 phases as prose in one response.
- Starts at **Phase 1 (Executive Summary)** and stops, waiting for the fellow.
  It does not pre-fill Phases 2–8 with guesses from the interview notes.
- Names which existing skills each phase draws on, and **only skills that exist
  here**: `problem-statement` and `problem-framing-canvas` for Phase 2,
  `proto-persona` / `jobs-to-be-done` for Phase 3, `user-story` +
  `user-story-splitting` for Phase 7. Says explicitly that Phase 7 has no
  epic-decomposition skill installed and the epic hypothesis is written inline.
- **Phase 2 requires evidence**, and the 9 interview notes are it. Distinguishes
  what the supervisors did (behaviour) from what they said they wanted (opinion)
  and weights accordingly.
- Names the artifact: the filled `template.md`, 10 sections, with the phase
  outputs behind each.

## Auto-fail if

Emits a complete 10-section PRD in one response (that is `create-prd`, and it
skips the alignment the fellow asked for); directs the fellow to
`epic-hypothesis`, `epic-breakdown-advisor` or `customer-journey-mapping-workshop`
(none exist here); invents supervisor quotes or retention numbers not supplied;
skips the facilitation protocol; or fails to state the day cost before starting.
