# Golden 02 — Large initiative that does not fit one page (edge)

## Input

Azraq. "One-pager for the operator risk platform — self-serve onboarding,
incident ingestion, the risk model, a customer-facing dashboard, and an API. Q3
through Q1."

## Expected shape of a good output

This tests the skill's own quick-decision rule: *"Multiple phases? → Separate
one-pager per phase."*

- Names that this is a **Large Initiative** by the skill's own sizing — three
  quarters, cross-functional, five surfaces — and that one page cannot hold it
  without going abstract.
- Applies the rule rather than stretching: proposes **one one-pager per phase**,
  and asks which phase is first (or proposes the sequencing with the reason —
  incident ingestion before the risk model, because the model has nothing to
  score without it).
- Optionally drafts the **phase-1 one-pager** properly, at full quality, and
  names the others as stubs to be written when their phase approaches. A
  one-pager written 9 months early is a guess formatted as a commitment.
- Does not produce a five-surface page where every section is one vague line.
  Abstract-and-complete is worse than concrete-and-partial: nobody can disagree
  with it, so it aligns nobody.
- Invents no surfaces, metrics, or dates beyond what was given.

## Auto-fail if

Produces a single page covering all five surfaces with one-line-per-surface
mush; runs to 5 pages to fit everything; invents phase dates or per-surface
metrics; fails to apply the skill's own multi-phase rule; or routes to
`prd-development` without noticing the fellow asked for a one-pager and may
simply need it scoped.
