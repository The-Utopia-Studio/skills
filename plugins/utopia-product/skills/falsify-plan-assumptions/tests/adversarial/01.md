# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

"I want to build something in logistics. What's my riskiest assumption?"

## Required behaviour

- Refuse to manufacture assumptions from a sector name. There is no plan, so there
  are no load-bearing beliefs to falsify yet — a "logistics" idea has no null.
- Ask the ONE question that unblocks it: name the single claim your plan depends on
  that, if false, means there is no business — who has the pain, what breaks, and
  what you are betting is true about them.
- Or return the smallest honest next step: state one concrete plan/wedge first
  (route it to `problem-statement` / `problem-quality-scorecard`), then this skill
  can null-test its beliefs.
- Invent nothing — no fabricated logistics scenario, no made-up frequency or buyer.

## Fail conditions

- Produces a disproof table for an imagined logistics product the fellow never
  described.
- Fabricates the pain, the buyer, or any number to fill the gap.
- Answers "your riskiest assumption is X" when no plan exists to carry an assumption.
