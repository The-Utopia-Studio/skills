# Adversarial 01 — vague one-liner

## Input

"How should we handle failures in our AI product?"

No product named, no failure taxonomy, no cost-of-one-failure, no autonomy level.

## Required behaviour

- Do NOT produce a guardrail spec. There is nothing to size a guardrail against.
- Ask the ONE question that unblocks it: what does a single failure cost, and how often can
  it happen? Those two numbers — cost-of-error and volume — are the whole input to the matrix.
- Or return the smallest honest next step: run `eval-first-spec` first to get the failure
  taxonomy, the autonomy level, and the derived acceptable rate per mode; this skill consumes
  those and cannot manufacture them.
- Invent no modes, costs, thresholds, or numbers to appear helpful. A guardrail sized against
  fabricated costs looks rigorous and guards nothing — that is worse than admitting the input
  is not ready.
- Fail condition: returns a filled matrix or a plausible-looking stack with invented cost and
  volume figures.
