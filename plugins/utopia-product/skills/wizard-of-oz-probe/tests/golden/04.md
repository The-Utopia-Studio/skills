# Golden 04 — mundane: fake the weekly summary email

**Input:** "Nothing dramatic — the product would auto-write a weekly summary email. For now just have someone write it by hand and see if anyone actually reads or forwards it."

**Expected shape of a good output:**
- Honest framing that even a low-stakes WoZ still enforces the fidelity contract: the human writes at the quality and cadence the auto-summary would, not a lovingly hand-crafted essay.
- Curtain: recipients think it's auto-generated; a human writes it. Opaque.
- 3–8 recipients; behaviour logged is open / read-through / forward / reply, not "did you like it."
- Read-out: engagement behaviour at 0.7 (opened and acted) vs 0.1 (said it was nice); "does NOT prove" block still present — this says nothing about whether the summary can be generated automatically, its cost, or scale.
- Does not inflate a mundane test into a demand or feasibility claim.
