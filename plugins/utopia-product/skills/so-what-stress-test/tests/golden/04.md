# Golden 04 — Barrier Intelligence: gas-safety alerts from field logs (REDESIGN)

## Input

One-liner: "Barrier Intelligence reads rig and plant field logs and sends gas-safety
alerts to the crew when readings drift toward danger."

Fellow's answers to the five:
1. So what? "A gas release event can shut a rig for days; the last near-miss at a partner
   site cost ~$300k in stand-down. Crews already keep manual gas logs every shift."
2. Who pays? "The HSE manager at the operator signs it — one operator already buys
   permit-to-work software from a competitor, so the budget line exists."
3. Wedge? "Crews get an alert on their phones when readings drift. It's advisory — they
   can act on it or not. We think they'll find it useful once they see it."
4. Moat? "We aggregate drift patterns across sites, and that dataset grows as more rigs
   join. Two sites feeding it now."
5. What kills it? "Regulators could mandate a specific certified sensor stack we don't
   integrate. Early signal: a draft standard in consultation. Mitigation: we're building
   the top three certified integrations now."

## Expected output shape

- Q1 **pass** — behaviour (0.7): a real stand-down cost and crews already logging by hand.
- Q2 **pass** — behaviour (0.7): named HSE manager, existing permit-software budget line.
- Q3 **weak** — the wedge is advisory. "They'll find it useful" is opinion (0.1); nothing
  forces adoption and there is no cost to ignoring an alert. This is the binding
  constraint.
- Q4 **pass** — behaviour (0.7): cross-site drift dataset already accruing from two sites.
- Q5 **pass** — killer named (mandated sensor stack), early signal (draft standard),
  mitigation underway (certified integrations).
- **Verdict: REDESIGN** — one weak row (Q3), no fatal.
- Weakest question: Q3 wedge.
- A **rewrite** that forces adoption: tie the alert into the permit-to-work sign-off, so a
  supervisor cannot close a permit while a gas-drift flag is open. That makes Barrier a
  gate in a mandatory workflow rather than an optional phone buzz. States the behaviour
  evidence to collect: one site running the permit-gated flow, and the sign-off refusal
  rate it produces.

Must NOT: pass the advisory wedge on "crews will find it useful". Must NOT hand off the
wedge rebuild to `wedge-five-questions` without first delivering the rewrite here — this
skill owns the whole-idea verdict; it may then suggest the deep-dive as a next step.
