# Feedback-program approvals and follow-through

Preserves the distinct coordination steps from the retired `stakeholder-ops` entrypoint. Use alongside the Power × Interest map for surveys, beta programs, listening tours and cross-team insight handoffs.

## Operating plan

1. Identify participating teams, decision rights, expectations at each stage and backup contacts. Mark unknown owners instead of inventing names.
2. Define approval checkpoints: brief sign-off, launch approval and readout review. Record required artifacts, accountable approver and due date.
3. Assign communication owners, audiences, channels and cadence. Draft updates without sending them unless the user authorizes sending.
4. Track follow-up actions with status, blockers and success criteria.
5. Agree review cadence and escalation paths. Automating reminders or modifying external systems requires the applicable authorization.
